Studio+ is available as monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions. The current localized price and billing period are shown in the app before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the purchase is confirmed.

Free Core Experience

Playback, manual review, review comparison and history, privacy controls, redacted report export, accessibility support, and deletion remain available without Studio+. Studio+ adds unlimited on-device automated reviews and monthly season chapters with grounded recaps; review comparison and history remain free.

Review Boundary

Ski Review Studio supports offline review of imported video and on-device observable trends. It does not provide real-time safety coaching, certified instruction, medical or injury-prevention advice, rescue services, hazard detection, objective skill scores, or right-or-wrong judging.
