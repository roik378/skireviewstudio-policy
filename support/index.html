What the App Does

Ski Review Studio is for offline imported-video review. It helps inspect user-selected ski clips, prepare capture guidance, and export redacted reports.

Account and Storage

The app does not require an account. Imported clips and review materials are stored locally on the device unless you choose to export or share a redacted report.

Studio+ Subscriptions

Purchases, renewals, refunds, and billing are handled by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in the app to recover an eligible purchase, or Manage Subscription to view or cancel a plan in your Apple Account.

Core playback, manual review, privacy controls, export, accessibility, and deletion remain available without Studio+.
