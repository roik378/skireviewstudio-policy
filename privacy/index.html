Ski Review Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

App: Ski Review Studio
Bundle ID: com.yi.skimvpapp

Summary

Ski Review Studio is an offline imported-video review app. This build does not collect personal data and does not track users. No account is required.

Local Video Review

When you import a ski clip, user-selected video stays on device for local trim, preflight, review, and redacted report preparation. The app does not send imported video, frame observations, report text, or app usage records to a developer server.

Data Collection

This build does not collect personal data from users. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, data broker services, or ad identifiers.

Subscriptions and Payments

Studio+ subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. Ski Review Studio does not receive or store payment card numbers, bank details, or Apple Account credentials. The app reads App Store transaction and entitlement status on device only to provide purchased features, restore purchases, show the active plan, and respond to expiration, revocation, or refund status.

Apple may process purchase and transaction information under Apple’s own privacy policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Accounts, Sync, and Location

No account is required. No server sync is included. No location capture is included. The app does not include cloud account sync, public sharing, or social discovery features.

Sharing and Export

The app can prepare redacted reports after the user chooses export or share actions. Source video and raw frame data are not included in the redacted report text.
